## What this says

Wrongness does **not** collapse onto one number. Bait-blindness is the harsh
axis: a half-right sim is discarded by the confirm. A sim that maximises the
two-day proxy is also discarded at φ=0, but a 50/50 mix with true V already
hands the confirm a quality-ish config.

Stale population (rotate topic tastes, keep the catalogue) is mild: even the
fully stale sim is within noise of B5. Miscalibrated rare-action predictions
barely matter, because ranking is dominated by like/dwell/share.

The docs/12 figure (five-point bait grid, crossover ≈ 0.75) is the one to
quote for missing features. The 0.98 on this table is the same bait axis on a
three-point grid that jumps from 0.5 → 1.0; it is not a new threshold.

Registered H3 (“there exists a fidelity below which M1 loses to B5”) holds
cleanly for bait and for pure-proxy. It does not hold for rare-rate error, and
barely holds for stale tastes.
